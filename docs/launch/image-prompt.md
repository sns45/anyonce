# Poster prompt for anyonce

Every text element below is taken from the case study (`content/anyonce/ARTICLE.md`, https://github.com/sns45/in8-home/pull/1). Do not let the image model add words: no "out now", no "production ready", no star counts, no "on npm".

````
Create a tall portrait neo-brutalist promotional poster for a developer tool called anyonce.

STYLE GOAL:
Use the exact same visual language as an indie hacker / retro software propaganda / hacker-zine / comic-book advertisement poster. The poster should feel loud, clever, crowded, high-energy, slightly chaotic, highly legible, playful, and handmade. It should look like a printed risograph / screenprint / comic-poster hybrid for a developer product launch.

FLEXIBLE CONTENT RULE:
All text groups below are variable-length inputs. Use as many as fit the composition while preserving clarity, hierarchy, density, and readability. If many items are provided, prioritize the strongest and shortest ones. Do not invent extra text.

FORMAT:
- Vertical poster, portrait orientation, aspect ratio 2:3 (1024x1536)
- Dense composition with many layered callouts
- Designed for social sharing on LinkedIn, Reddit and GitHub
- Strong focal hierarchy: giant title, central illustration, supporting badges, bold CTA

CORE COLOR SYSTEM:
Saturated Van Leeuwen ice cream shop palette, pushed for poster impact.
Background yellows: butter yellow #F4D83D, creamy bright yellow #F6E65B, golden shop-wall yellow #E7CF3A, warm honey yellow #D8B64A.
Contrast: deep black #111111, soft charcoal #2B2B2B, cream #F7F1DD.
Accents: hot red #E94235, orange-red #F05A2A, neon green #7DFF3A, cyan #6ED8FF, occasional coffee brown #5A3A22 and caramel #B8844A.
Push brightness and saturation beyond natural photography; keep blacks deep and graphic; avoid muted, pastel, beige or corporate-neutral palettes.

BACKGROUND:
Bright butter-yellow field with heavy paper grain, ink noise, halftone wear, subtle scratches and imperfect print aging, with slight tonal variation so it feels printed and alive.

COMPOSITION:
1. huge title at the top
2. one strong central illustration
3. sticker-like banners and rectangular callout boxes around it
4. a red starburst badge upper-right
5. a bold slogan band in the middle
6. a large CTA strip near the bottom
7. a footer with languages, stores and standards
8. asymmetrical but balanced, overlapping panels, angled labels, dense but with clear margins

TYPOGRAPHY:
Oversized bold sans-serif display type for the title, condensed bold uppercase for labels, a few tilted hand-lettered captions. Comic-book ad and hacker flyer energy, readable, not polished.

TITLE TREATMENT:
Main title "anyonce" as a giant red-orange stacked headline with a thick black outline and printed drop shadow.
Top banner strip above it: "OPEN SOURCE, TYPESCRIPT AND GO"
Subtitle below it: "One idempotency core for HTTP, queues and webhooks"

MAIN VISUAL / HERO ILLUSTRATION:
A retro comic-book coat-check attendant behind a wooden counter (caramel wood tone), wearing a visor, facing three doors labelled HTTP, QUEUE and WEBHOOK. From each door a duplicate courier arrives waving the SAME numbered ticket "#order-1". The attendant hands out exactly one coat and stamps the other tickets "REPLAYED", while a second courier waving a ticket with a different coat description gets a big red "422: NOT YOUR COAT" stamp. Hand-drawn, graphic novel style, thick outlines, posterized shading, limited bold colors, textured ink, semi-cartoonish vintage commercial illustration.

SUPPORTING OBJECTS:
A rubber stamp reading "409 WAIT", a stopwatch labelled "LEASE", a numbered brass token labelled "FENCE +1", a padlock snapping shut on a single ticket stub, a wax-sealed envelope labelled "webhook-id" with a signature checkmark, a conveyor belt of parcels labelled "redelivery", a small terminal window.

UI / TERMINAL / PRODUCT PANELS:
Terminal snippets, short and real:
- "Idempotency-Key: order-1"
- "HTTP/1.1 201 Created / Idempotency-Replayed: true"
- "422 fingerprint-mismatch"
- "20 passed, 0 failed"

BADGES / STICKERS / CALLOUT BOXES:
Varied colored rectangular stickers (cyan with black text, red with cream text, black with yellow text, green with black text, cream with black text):
- "ONE ATOMIC WRITE"
- "50 RACERS, 1 WINNER"
- "3 DOORS, 1 STATE MACHINE"
- "20 CONFORMANCE VECTORS"
- "VERIFY, THEN CLAIM"
- "RFC 9457 PROBLEMS"

STARBURST BADGE:
Upper-right red/orange burst with thick black outline and cream text: "0.1.0 PRE-RELEASE"

MID-POSTER VALUE PROPOSITION:
Dark rounded pill band across the middle: "THE SAME TICKET GETS THE SAME COAT"

FEATURE LIST:
Starred ad-copy lines beside the counter:
- "Retry? Same answer, handler runs once"
- "Still running? 409 with Retry-After"
- "Different payload? 422"
- "5xx releases the key"

STATUS LABELS / MICRO-CALLOUTS:
Tiny chips and corner notes: "PRE-RELEASE", "CI GREEN", "NO KV STORE, BY DESIGN", "BYTE-IDENTICAL IN TS AND GO", "AT MOST ONCE WHILE THE RECORD LIVES", "17 DRAFT GAPS FOUND"

BOTTOM CTA STRIP:
Wide black-bordered cream strip: "READ THE CASE STUDY: in8.sh/work/anyonce"

BOTTOM SUPPORTING DETAILS / INTEGRATIONS:
Footer band: "TypeScript + Go", "Hono", "anyq", "Standard Webhooks", "Postgres", "Redis", "DynamoDB", "IETF Idempotency-Key draft", "github.com/sns45/anyonce"

MOOD / TONE:
Clever, hacker-friendly, witty, slightly mischievous, confident, handcrafted, vintage print, maximalist but controlled.

TEXTURE + FINISH:
Strong paper grain, distressed ink, halftone, rough edges, imperfect alignment, slight aging, tiny arrows, stars and motion marks. No gradients, glassmorphism or SaaS polish.

NEGATIVE PROMPT / AVOID:
Minimal, corporate, glossy, photorealistic, 3D render, pastel, thin type, empty or symmetrical layouts, generic conference flyer, generic AI-poster look. Do not add any text not listed above, and never the words "production ready", "out now", "available on npm", "stars" or "downloads".
````
